How to Play(A short introduction to the game)

The Objective of Chess ♟

Chess is a two-player strategy game played on an 8x8 board of 64 squares. The main objective is simple: checkmate your opponent's King (as shown in the pic), which means putting the King in a position where it is under attack and has no safe squares to move to.

1 How the pieces move

1

The Pawn ♟

Moves forward 1 square at a time (or 2 squares on its very first move). It captures opponent pieces 1 square diagonally forward.

2

The Knight ♞

Moves in an "L" shape (2 squares in one direction, then 1 square perpendicular). The Knight is the only piece on the board that can jump over other pieces!

3

The Bishop ♝

Moves diagonally as many open squares as it wants, staying on its starting color for the entire game.

4

The Rook ♜

Moves horizontally or vertically across any number of open squares in a straight line.

5

The Queen ♛

The most powerful piece on the board! She can move in any straight direction—forward, backward, sideways, or diagonally—as far as the path is clear.

6

The King ♚

The most critical piece to protect. He can move 1 square in any direction. If your King is trapped with no legal moves, the game is over.

2 The 3 stages of the Game

1

The Opening

The start of the game where you control the center squares, develop your knights and bishops, and castle your king for safety.

2

The Middlegame

The battle phase! Players execute tactical attacks, capture enemy pieces, open up lines, and build plans to break down the opponent's defense.

3

The Endgame

Only a few pieces remain on the board. The main goals are pushing pawns to promote them into Queens and delivering the final checkmate.

The spark that changed it all✨

My mother showed me a video of the 2022 Asian Continental Championship where R Praggnanandhaa finished with 7 points in 9 matches which helped him score Gold in the tournament. I was inspired by his very smart plays and hence wanted to play like him. As chess was starting to become much more popular than before, I started to pick up the sport.

2

My First Lessons📚🍎

My mother, who had seen my interest in chess, had decided to add me into a chess academy where I get to meet up with other like-minded people with similar interests. Since the academy was overseas, my coach had decided to do online meetings. I first had to take a small course on how to play the game (how the pieces move etc.)

3
